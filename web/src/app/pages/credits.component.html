BEHIND THE CONNECTIONS

DATA.
NOT GUESSWORK.

The Last Link connects real credits through stable identities. Every match uses one published catalogue version.

MOVIES / THE MOVIE DATABASE

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Movie data comes from TMDB, through Kaggle’s TMDB 5000 archive, version 2, dated 28 September 2017. This release imports 4,803 films and publishes 3,505 after automated validation. It includes all eligible credits in the source, rather than a top-three cast selection. It is an archived selection, not a current or exhaustive film database.

Connections use actors (including credited voices), directors, writers, original score composers and cinematographers. Writers are mapped from Writer, Screenplay, Teleplay, Novel, Book and Short Story. Original Music Composer and Director of Photography are the precise score and camera jobs. Explicitly uncredited cast appearances are excluded.

The same TMDB person ID has one identity across eligible roles and a shared three-use allowance. A role need not be the same on both films. Names, titles, genres and fictional characters never create movie connections.

Missing source credits remain unknown. Automated validation checks structure and eligibility; it does not independently verify every screen credit. See the project’s MOVIES_DATA_REPORT.md and provenance database for the complete audit.

TMDB’s free data use is for noncommercial projects with attribution. Commercial use requires the appropriate TMDB agreement. The archive does not grant a new unrestricted licence.

GAMES / REVIEWED CREDITS

The preserved 35-game playable catalogue uses selected, reviewed credits backed by Wikidata and cited Wikipedia sources. Wikidata structured data is CC0. Wikipedia-derived facts and additions retain English Wikipedia contributor attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 provenance in the project. The 200-game research collection remains separate from play.

FOOTBALL / WIKIDATA

Ninety curated major senior men’s clubs from around the world. National teams, reserve teams and youth teams are excluded. Historical players and head managers create connections, including a player at one club who managed another. Each person has one shared three-use allowance.

Structured career facts come from Wikidata under CC0, collected on 28 October 2026 through the public QLever Wikidata endpoint. Player memberships require a positive recorded appearance count; manager appointments use the head-coach property. Caretakers qualify. Directors and unspecified coaching jobs do not.

Career coverage is incomplete, especially for managers. Unknown or zero appearance counts and uncertain roles are excluded instead of guessed. Some real connections may therefore be missing. Automated checks confirm structure and eligibility, not every historical career. The project includes statement identities, dates where available, source links and a coverage audit. No club crests or licensed photographs are used.

FAMILIAR FAVOURITES / JUST THE FIRST LINK

Movie and Football hosts can choose a curated pool of recognizable starters. These are editorial selections, not live popularity rankings. The server randomly selects the opening from the saved pool. All published entries remain available for subsequent answers, with the same timer and connection rules.
