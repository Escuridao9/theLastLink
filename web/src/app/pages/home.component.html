THE SHORT VERSION

Easy to learn.
Hard to get out of.

Two players. One chain. Run out of time and you lose.

  1. 01

    Pick an arena. Invite a friend.

    Choose Movies, Games or Football Clubs, then share your private room code. The server picks the opening entry.

  2. 02

    Make a link in 20 seconds.

    Answer with an unplayed entry in the same category. Movies share cast or eligible crew; games share developers, composers, voice actors or characters; clubs share players or managers. A valid answer starts your opponent’s 20 seconds. Invalid? Keep trying on the same clock.

  3. 03

    Use your links. Outlast your opponent.

    Every shared connector counts, with three uses each across both players. Any exhausted connector blocks the move. No repeats. Traps welcome. Whoever runs out of time loses.
