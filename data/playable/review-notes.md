# Catalogue v1 review boundary

The explicit allowlist in `scripts/build-playable.ts` approves selected records only. The generated approval manifest retains each original assertion and its pinned source evidence. Source assertion strength and approval for this finite game are different fields. Approval does not mean independently verified primary-source evidence or exhaustive credits.

This review checked canonical identities, same-role eligibility, original developer versus publisher/port credits, and named recurring characters. Developers ambiguous at the studio/division level and port-only companies were omitted. Character entities derived from unspecified “additional voices”, generic crowds, objects and mythological identities shared ambiguously between fictional universes were not approved. Super Smash Bros. Ultimate's selected fighters include released DLC (Cloud, Sephiroth and Sora).

Held out: **Kuniyuki Takahashi, NieR: Automata composer**. The research snapshot contains two different canonical IDs with this label. Neither is approved until their identity is resolved; the build rejects ambiguous name matches. The complete original assertions remain in the research snapshot. Tai Tomisawa and Nobuyoshi Suzuki remain unresolved research exclusions as before.

The catalogue is a bounded game definition, not a promise that an unlisted real-world connection does not exist. Both players can inspect every eligible published credit before play. All published shared connectors must be consumed together.
